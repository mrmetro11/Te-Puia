What is Te Puia?

Welcome to Te Puia! Te Puia is a popular cultural and geothermal center in Rotorua, New Zealand. Located in Whakarewarewa Thermal Valley. Spanning a vibrant landscape alive with active geysers, bubbling mud pools. The New Zealand Māori Arts and Crafts institute, and a Kiwi consevation center. Te Puia has 500+ Pools, 65 Geyser Vents, 60-70 Hectares of land!
